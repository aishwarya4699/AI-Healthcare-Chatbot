SAMPLE HEALTH PLAN - PHARMACY PRIOR AUTHORIZATION POLICY (SYNTHETIC, FOR DEMO ONLY)
Policy ID: RX-318
Title: Isotretinoin (oral) for Acne
Effective date: 01/01/2026

Purpose:
This policy defines when oral isotretinoin is covered for members with acne.

Approval criteria:
Oral isotretinoin is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are documented:

Criterion 1 - Severity: Diagnosis of severe recalcitrant nodular (nodulocystic) acne, OR moderate acne that is causing scarring.

Criterion 2 - Prior treatment failure: The member has tried and failed at least 3 months (12 weeks) of an oral antibiotic (for example doxycycline or minocycline) used together with a topical therapy (for example a topical retinoid such as tretinoin, or benzoyl peroxide).

Criterion 3 - Safety program: The prescriber and the member are registered in the iPLEDGE REMS program.

Criterion 4 - Pregnancy prevention: For members who can become pregnant, a negative pregnancy test and two forms of contraception are documented. This criterion is not applicable to members who cannot become pregnant.

Approval duration:
Initial approval is for one course of up to 20 weeks.

Not medically necessary:
Mild or moderate acne without scarring that has not been treated with an oral antibiotic plus topical therapy for at least 3 months.

Review process:
Requests that do not clearly meet criteria are pended for review by a licensed clinical pharmacist or physician reviewer. Only a physician reviewer may issue an adverse determination.
